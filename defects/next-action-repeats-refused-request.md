Three results name as their next action the request that was just refused, or none:
`ethos adopt --select-runtime` where the refused selection carries no repair, a refused rename or
recovery of a retained topic under `ethos lane repair-paradigm`, and a failed source-format gate
inside a proof, whose next action is `ethos plan --changed --json` where the command form names the
format apply. Read the result's `required_gaps` for the cause.
