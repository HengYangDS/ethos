# DR-0035: Ten Rows of the Release Candidate Gate Are Cut to the Parts That 0.2.0 Requires

Purpose: preserve the scope resolution of 2026-11-17: the part of each of ten rows that 0.2.0
requires, the remainders that follow it, two waivers, the condition for arming reuse, and the
corrections of the plan's words.

See also: [DR-0026: The 0.2.0 Release Candidate Gate Is Recorded With Three
Waivers](dr-0026-release-candidate-gate.md), whose items the rows come from, and [DR-0029: The
Release Candidate Gate Gains the Law, and Its Dates Become Earliest
Dates](dr-0029-release-candidate-gate-successor.md), which says that a scope cut is a successor
record.

## Context

DR-0029 holds the gate of 0.2.0 as 43 rows with five weekly tags. Design studies of 2026-11-17
for the batch of 0.2.0-beta.13 found the plan's rows larger than the sizes the plan gave them: of
nine studied rows only a first slice fits its batch, and the first slices alone pass the test
ceiling. The release lead brought the smallest proposal, to move out only the parts that cannot
take effect inside the freeze. The owner sent the question to the committee the same day. Five
seats answered in three rounds: four signed the second text, the adversary seat blocked it with
two exact changes, and all five signed the third. The owner answered its five asks that evening,
each with the committee's recommendation.

## Decision

- **DR-0029 stands except as written here.** R14, done, the cut, the copies run, the freeze, the
  tags and the slip order are unchanged. The measure stays 43 rows and no slice is a row. This
  record cuts scope; it arms no refusal and flips no default before 0.2.0. A rule that enters in
  the reporting state and changes no verdict is not a new rule of the freeze.
- **Rows by the gate's words.** For each row below, 0.2.0 requires the stated part. A named
  remainder follows 0.2.0 and holds no row open: by a line here where its item's sentence in
  DR-0026 holds without it, by a waiver where it does not. A row is done when every part kept here
  is archived on `dev` under a passed full proof. None of these ten rows is done today.
- **Stated parts.** `fast-tier`: readiness reports the selection, and a command runs it under a
  host ticket. `gate-environment`: the report, the cache root, and the contract's sentence on how
  a declared command is launched. `rollback-after-repair`: the journey records `admitted` or
  `refused` as the product decides, and a pinned tree's rollback writes its receipt.
  `residue-class`: whole, as one unit. `release-rehearsal`: platforms, assembly and the journey on
  the assembly. `rehearsal-admission`: the rule reports, and ETHOS's binding opts in once its pin
  reads the key. `gate-reuse`: key, record, comparison, and the arming of gates whose key is
  closed and that start no child but Git. `store-retention`: the release of an absent consumer,
  the store report, `status` names an over row, two observed windows. `proof-output-scratch`: the
  reader, the footprint report, the registry's move. `release-provenance`: whole, unread.
- **Follows 0.2.0.** The fast tier's late gates and its selection from recorded facts. The
  list-built child as a repository's state, with output redaction and the Git overlay keys; its
  default follows the state by one release. The list for a detached run's runner goes with
  it. The landings of a pin and of its revert, and the pin followed through Mise. The split of the
  test gate. Three record kinds: publication requests, superseded units, the retention
  verb's own plans and receipts. A dead owner's scratch.
- **Waiver 4**. Waived, of R4: "and tag admission requires its receipt". 0.2.0 then lacks
  a refusal of an unrehearsed release, the bound receipt, its Attestation, its command and the
  requirement that ETHOS opts in. In its place a rehearsal runs before each tag from the first tag
  after its three parts land, the rule reports at the publication probe of each tag after ETHOS's
  binding opts in, each release Change holds a task that reads the verdict, and a second session
  reads the run. The refusal arms no earlier than 0.3.0, after a release that reported it.
- **A waiver of R7, on a condition**. If `residue-class` has no patch measured at the cut
  of 2026-12-03, it leaves whole that day. Waived then, of R7: "and a landed lane retires with
  declared, natively checked residue". 0.2.0 then lacks the review derived at a landed retirement;
  the review by hand stays in its place. The measure then reads 42 rows, and the page names the
  row as left by waiver, never done.
- **Arming.** A gate's result is reused only on its own record, at a threshold written before the
  first comparison is counted, with records from three governed repositories; the Attestation
  names a reused result. No gate arms in the frozen beta. No gate whose inputs are not closed arms
  before 0.2.0.
- **Lines and cuts.** No tag and no line changes. When the freeze moves one week, the line read
  that day and each later line move one tag day. 2027-01-30 is no longer stated as a bound: one is
  printed from two tag-day readings of `dev`, and a row with no study at its cut is unsized (today
  fifteen of the 24 rows that follow). A kept part names the cut that measures its patch,
  2026-12-03 or 12-10.
- **Words corrected.** No published predecessor since 0.2.0-beta.6 refuses a rollback after a
  repair; the open state is `runtime_selection_open`. No rehearsal follows a pin to an unpublished
  candidate; its Attestation is `release:rehearsal`. The fast tier adds no gate to readiness. Byte
  budgets report and refuse nothing. Of D.1 to D.4, three record kinds and the `status` sentence
  were not built. No key makes a product bump cheap. No cold proof meets 900 seconds: that text is
  amended by the frozen beta with a figure measured on the reference host. A declared command
  resolves on the launch path, and the contract says so.
- **The entry and the page.** The 0.2.0 entry lists each remainder that left with what a
  repository does by hand in its place, the seconds that reuse saved as measured, and that byte
  budgets report, with the reading of ETHOS's own stores beside each budget. The weekly page names
  each open row's slice and each remainder that left. Another session reads the rows of beta.11
  and beta.12 against DR-0026 by 12-05. Before the candidate, on a copy of one governed tree and a
  published pair of releases, a pin's bump is landed, rolled back with a reason, and its revert
  landed under the predecessor; the weekly page holds the result and the host minutes.

## Alternatives Rejected

- A cut by size. Size and time may be why a remainder cannot land, and they are never the
  written reason; a remainder leaves by name, as a cut of scope.
- The smallest proposal as worded, by which a part leaves as an effect of the freeze. What
  leaves a sentence of DR-0026 false leaves by a named waiver.
- Refusing Waiver 4, by which R4 holds the candidate until a refusal of an unrehearsed release
  is built.
- Counting a report as a new rule of the freeze, by which three reports leave the freeze and two
  decisions of the same day are taken back.
- Keeping the split of the test gate in 0.2.0: about 1,111 seconds saved by estimate, armed in
  the frozen beta against the kernel seat's condition.
- Letting `residue-class` leave at once, or keeping it with no exit, by which a miss slips and
  may move the freeze.
- The list-built child as a repository's state in the batch of 0.2.0-beta.14, with a flip of its
  default at the first release of 0.3.0.
- Redrawing the lines by the landing rate, and keeping 2027-01-30 beside each date: the rate was
  measured once, and rows read two to nine times their plan size in tests.

## Consequences and Boundary

- The record changes no product behavior. It arms no refusal and flips no default before 0.2.0.
- The measure stays 43 rows, and reads 42 only by the waiver of R7 on its condition. A slice is
  never counted as a row.
- 0.2.0 lacks what the waivers name: a refusal of an unrehearsed release and, on the condition,
  the review derived at a landed retirement. Each has a named replacement by hand.
- The contract's sentence on how a declared command is launched changed with the Change of
  `gate-environment`, landed on 2026-11-21: the page holds the sentence that the owner approved.
- DR-0026, DR-0029 and the archived plan keep their text. Each of the two records carries the
  mark of this one where it adds to or replaces a sentence.
- This record adds no checker. A later waiver, a row that leaves or a further cut is a successor
  record that names this one.

## Evidence and Revisit

- The committee's resolution and its votes are local documents of the releasing checkout; the
  design of the Change that lands this record is their tracked form, with the test that decided
  each row, where the five seats differed and how each difference was settled.
- Every size but one is an estimate of the studies: only the first slice of `fast-tier` was
  measured when the committee sat. No seat ran a test, a proof or a spike; no seat read the study
  of `residue-class`, and fifteen of the 24 rows that follow have none.
- The forecast for 2026-12-05 is an inference: `dev` reads 22 to 24 rows against a line of 15 if
  the nine rows of 0.2.0-beta.12 have landed, on which the slip rule moves the freeze one week.
- The sentence on a report inside the freeze and the parts of the waiver of R7 are the clerk's
  wording, signed by all five seats.

Revisit at the cuts of 2026-12-03 and 2026-12-10, where each kept part's patch is measured and the
owner reads the product ceiling, and by 2026-12-05, when another session reads the rows of
0.2.0-beta.11 and 0.2.0-beta.12 against DR-0026.
