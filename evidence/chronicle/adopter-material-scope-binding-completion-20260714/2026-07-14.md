# Authoritative adopter material scope binding — 2026-07-14

## Scope

This active Change productizes the ETHOS-owned companion contract that binds
adopter-declared material paths to an official OpenSpec Change. It extends the
already accepted universal lifecycle behavior: `plan` and `prove` remain on the
official OpenSpec lifecycle; this record does not replace or duplicate that
lifecycle.

## Boundary

`[openspec].material_paths` and Change-local `scope.toml` are portable,
repository-governance declarations. The companion is not official OpenSpec
workflow-schema metadata. Lifecycle scope is not a code-correctness gate, and
Superpowers or any other method package is not a governance carrier.

`lane prewrite`, `plan --changed`, and `prove` consume the same lifecycle scope
read model. An uncovered material path reports
`openspec_material_path_uncovered:<path>`. The only bootstrap admission is an
official active Change's exact absent `scope.toml`; after creation it must cover
itself. Missing or invalid unrelated companions remain diagnostics rather than
global coverage blockers.
