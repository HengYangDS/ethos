# Archive transition scope admission — 2026-07-15

## Scope

This Change repairs a product gap discovered while closing the DDWG governance
correction: once the official OpenSpec archive command moves the active carrier,
the final archive files and claim repoint no longer have an active companion to
cover their material paths.

## Decision

ETHOS may read an archived companion only when a path beneath that exact archive
is already part of the current Work Lane diff. The companion can then cover only
requested material paths matching its declared scope. An archive not present in
the current diff remains historical evidence, not standing admission authority.

## Pre-commit evidence

The focused archive-scope and invalid-state tests passed (20 tests). Strict
OpenSpec validation, repository lifecycle validation, and changed planning also
passed on the uncommitted Work Lane state. A HEAD-bound executed proof remains a
post-commit requirement before archive; this note does not treat a pre-commit
result as proof.

The DDWG archive-repoint commit is a consumer of this mechanism, not proof that
this ETHOS Change has landed or been published.

## Non-claims

This record does not claim candidate landing, accepted-root closeout, remote
publication, hosted CI, or a change to official OpenSpec archive semantics.
