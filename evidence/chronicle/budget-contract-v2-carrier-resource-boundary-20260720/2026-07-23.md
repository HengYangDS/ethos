## Implemented Boundary

The candidate advances the metric registry to wire version 4 and binds every
provider to one descriptor-owned execution tuple. UTF-8 footprint/control and
the finite diagram grammar remain on the reviewed bounded path. Python, JSON,
TOML, YAML, INI, Jinja, and shell use one carrier per isolated invocation with
no fallback to the bounded path.

The parent resolves the complete provider and execution identity before content
open, rejects an initial oversize object before the first content read, retains
one bounded content object, and rechecks direct bytes before dispatch. The
worker protocol is typed, canonical, length-framed, path-blind, and replayed by
the parent before a measurement is exposed. Bootstrap, supervisor, platform,
exchange, and lifecycle ownership are split into bounded modules; cleanup has
one owner, bounded retries, stable first-cause precedence, and complete
all-or-nothing result semantics.
