## Intended Measurement Contract

The active design requires strict frozen and self-verifying native, carrier,
coordinate, and snapshot models. Every load envelope has an exact XOR between a
typed success and stable required gaps. Exact raw content SHA-256 remains
separate from normalized/provider/vector identity.

Worktree reads traverse each path component relative to a repository descriptor
with no-follow semantics, require a regular final file, and compare device,
inode, mode, size, mtime, and ctime before and after reading. Those OS facts are
ephemeral drift checks and never enter persistent digests. Any file, parser,
signature, runtime-conformance, or state failure rejects the complete carrier
or snapshot result; partial clean vectors are forbidden. Direct carrier
measurement accepts only classified measured identities. Snapshot aggregation
skips reviewed exclusions while binding the complete manifest and inventory
digests, so exclusions remain visible in snapshot identity.

Native providers cover UTF-8 footprint/control, Python, TOML, JSON, YAML, INI,
Jinja parse-only structure, a finite repository-owned Bash/Zsh-capable shell
lexer, and the finite repository C4 grammar. Duplicate keys, non-finite values,
unsafe or cyclic YAML structures, invalid UTF-8, malformed syntax, unknown
signatures, and unavailable providers are required gaps. Model/BPE tokens remain
confined to agent-runtime budgets.
