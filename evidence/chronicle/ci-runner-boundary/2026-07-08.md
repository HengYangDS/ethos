# CI Runner Boundary Evidence — 2026-07-08

## Claim

ETHOS separates configuration from execution for CI and quality gates: tool-native
policy lives under `.config/checks/**`, while reusable repository-owned runner
scripts live under `tools/ci/scripts/**`.

## Evidence

- Runner scripts were moved from `.config/ci/scripts/` to `tools/ci/scripts/`.
- `.gitlab-ci.yml`, `.pre-commit-config.yaml`, `system/tools.toml`, quality gate
  descriptors, skills, and tests were updated to invoke `tools/ci/scripts/`.
- Generated-artifact topology admits `tools/ci/scripts/` as reusable runner
  tooling and marks `.config/ci/scripts/` as a retired runner home.
- Local fallback evidence remains explicitly local; it does not assert hosted CI
  success or remote publication.
