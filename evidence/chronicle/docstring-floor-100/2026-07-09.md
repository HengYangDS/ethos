# Docstring Floor 100 Evidence

## Claim

ETHOS enforces the current 100 percent public Python product-surface docstring
coverage as the policy floor instead of preserving a stale 95 percent tolerance.

## Net gain

- Achieved docstring coverage becomes the enforced floor.
- New public product surfaces must carry intent-bearing Google-style docstrings.
- The broader public-definition inventory remains advisory, so private helpers do
  not become a second documentation store.

## Checks

Initial checks in this Work Lane:

- TDD red: `uv run --group dev pytest -q tests/architecture/test_docstring_governance.py::test_docstring_policy_admits_google_style_with_signature_checks` failed with `assert 95 == 100` before the policy change.
- TDD green: focused architecture and policy tests passed after raising the policy floor.
- Docstring gate: `uv run --package ethos ethos quality docstrings --json` returned `ok=true`, `state=clean`, `fail_under=100.0`, and `coverage_percent=100.0`.

## Boundaries
