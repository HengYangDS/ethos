# Docstring Floor 100 Evidence

## Claim

ETHOS enforces the current 100 percent public Python product-surface docstring
coverage as the policy floor instead of preserving a stale 95 percent tolerance.

## Net gain

- Achieved docstring coverage becomes the enforced floor.
- New public product surfaces must carry intent-bearing Google-style docstrings.
- The broader public-definition inventory remains advisory, so private helpers do
  not become a second documentation store.
