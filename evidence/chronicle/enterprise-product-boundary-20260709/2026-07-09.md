# Enterprise Product Boundary Evidence

## Claim

ETHOS product-boundary governance is enterprise-neutral: active product
surfaces, release metadata, contributor policy, distribution package scope, and
active product planning surfaces remain organization-native rather than
person-native, workstation-native, private-reference-native, or adopter-private.

## Trigger signal

A general-purpose enterprise product cannot ship with hardcoded author identity,
private workstation paths, adopter-private names, or package metadata that makes
a single individual the product authority. Multi-contributor organizations need
role-based identity facts and package-manager distributions that publish only
neutral launcher assets.

A second trigger was active planning residue: roadmap, rule, and local `.ethos`
configuration surfaces must not cite named private repositories or personal work
history as product authority. Such references are historical facts or research
inputs at most; they do not belong in active product defaults for broad
enterprise adoption.

## Mechanism

- `ethos quality product-boundary --json` audits distribution manifests in the
  same product-boundary gate that already checks active product surfaces and
  release metadata.
- NPM distribution manifests must use an explicit `files` allowlist limited to
  neutral launcher assets and package documentation.
- Product package metadata rejects `author`, `authors`, `maintainers`, and
  `contributors` person attribution fields.
- The root workspace package must remain non-publishable when it declares
  package-manager workspaces.
- The product-boundary gate rejects active product plans, rules, and
  configuration comments that make a named private repository or personal work
  history a product authority. The implementation checks the leak shape rather
  than hardcoding any private adopter name into ETHOS.
- The tracked local `.ethos/quality-regime-decision.md` personal/reference
  comparison record was removed from active product surfaces; retained mechanism
  value was compressed into neutral rules, configuration comments, and a generic
  tooling adoption roadmap.
- `ethos quality contributor-policy --json` requires product repositories to
  use external role policy and reports distinct identity facts: Git author, Git
  committer, Work Lane actor, reviewer, maintainer, bot, team, and adopter-side
  owner.
- OpenSpec quality and distribution specs carry the promoted requirements; the
  product design contract, distribution architecture docs, tooling roadmap, and
  reference documentation state the enterprise boundary in canonical prose.
- After refreshing against the candidate train, the same boundary was extended to active reference documentation: a newly accepted mechanism-comparison reference was rewritten into mechanism-family language, and the gate now rejects explicit named-reference comparison, snapshot, reference-checkout, and comparison-table shapes without hardcoding any private repository name.

- The invalid-state taxonomy now classifies product-boundary distribution, identity-mode, and private-reference gaps under the Commitment node, preserving the MECE gap model.
- Generic parity evidence was refreshed after this product-boundary semantic change so `ethos parity gaps --adopter generic --json` is clean for the lane head.

## Boundary

repository migration. Historical evidence, archived change records, and scoped
research notes may retain factual historical names. Active product surfaces,
package defaults, distribution manifests, active plans/rules/configuration
comments, and organization identity policy remain neutral and role-based.
