# Evidence Evolution Protocol - 2026-07-08

## Boundary

This change strengthens the existing Evidence / Claim / Chronicle / Evolution
loop. It does not add a new proof root, ledger, command plane, or vendor
surface. Claims remain under `evidence/claims/`, judged history remains under
`evidence/chronicle/`, and structural evolution remains under
`evolution/ledger.toml`.

## Changes

- `evolution_report` now reports gaps when non-campaign evolution entries lack
  evidence or decision refs, when entry refs are not arrays, when hypothesis
  proof/review/decision refs are not arrays, or when hypothesis refs do not
  resolve.
- `system/invalid_states.toml` and the packaged `ethos_core` taxonomy resource
  classify the new evolution protocol gaps under Chronicle so every emitted gap
  still reduces to exactly one kernel-derived invalid state.
- `ethos quality evidence-freshness --json` now reports both claim freshness and
  evolution protocol health.
- The default product and adopter proof floors include the trust-bearing
  `evidence-freshness` gate.
- `evolution-ledger.schema.json` now requires structural entries to bind
  evidence and decision refs while keeping campaign container entries lightweight.
