# Campaign-Terminal Publication Gate Evidence

## Fact

The global compression program spans multiple locally closed OpenSpec Changes.
Per-Change remote closeout and terminal-budget enforcement made that long-running
program slower without proving its final net deletion.

## Decision

One campaign manifest now declares the local protected-publication boundary.
Local Change proof, archive, candidate landing, and accepted-root closeout remain
independent transitions. The local pre-push reducer blocks protected destinations
until campaign lifecycle, source-budget terminal targets, and zero active debt
agree. Hosted receiving-plane protection remains authoritative.

`campaign_terminal` defers current-size and terminal-target enforcement while
retaining explicit measurement, debt-cap validation, and debt lifecycle failures.
No baseline or aggregate debt maximum was increased by this Change. Expired
historical debt records were removed rather than extended; the active maximum
fell from 8,163 to 165 effective lines while current growth remains explicit.

## Evidence Boundary

This record supports the bounded mechanism and its local tests. It does not claim
campaign completion, terminal source-budget attainment, remote publication, or
hosted acceptance. Those remain explicitly open until the compression Campaign
reaches its terminal state.
