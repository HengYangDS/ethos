# Land role boundary

ETHOS corrected the normal land readiness surface so protected roots no longer
report `ready_to_land` for `ethos land --json`. Normal land remains the Work Lane
to candidate transition. Accepted-root promotion remains `ethos land --closeout`.

Initial regression tests failed on accepted-root and candidate dry-run land. The
mutation decision reducer now evaluates role and dirty-state gaps for dry-run
land while reserving authorization, expect-head, and executed-proof checks for
apply land.
