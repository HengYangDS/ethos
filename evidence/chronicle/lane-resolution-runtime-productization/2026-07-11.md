## Judgment

The authorized hardening is implemented as a bounded local product change:

- successful exceptional resolutions materialize a schema-validated receipt at
  `build/artifacts/lane-resolution/receipts/<sha256(decision-id)>.json`;
- inventory derives retained, cleared, receipt-only, and unindexed states from
  receipts, manifests, and clear receipts without treating any artifact as
  authority;
- retention clear requires the exact manifest SHA-256, a Chronicle containing
  `lane_resolution/clear-preservation`, a reason, break-glass, and irreversible
  confirmation before it records a clear receipt and removes one package;
- new Work Lanes receive a source-bound runner contract whose uv environment
  and cache live in `build/runtime/venv` and
  `build/runtime/tool-cache/uv`.

Receipt paths are created exclusively: a second write for the same decision
fails rather than overwriting the first receipt. Existing preservation packages
remain visible as unindexed until a future explicit reconciliation; none are
migrated or deleted by this change.
