## Boundary

Logical Change IDs now mean date-free lower-kebab identifiers beginning with a
letter. An archive has one date only, in
`openspec/changes/archive/YYYY-MM-DD-<change-id>/`. Claim IDs and Chronicle
dates remain evidence identities, not Change IDs. Campaign manifests use the same
date-free logical IDs; their Claim IDs remain dated evidence identities.

## Implementation

All 137 observed non-canonical archive carriers were renamed to the canonical
single-date archive form. Tracked references and declared logical `change_id`
fields were normalized. The two distinct current-head adopter observations
remain distinct logical IDs: `current-product-head-real-adopter-evidence` and
`current-candidate-head-real-adopter-evidence`.
A non-canonical archive carrier introduced on the candidate train during this
lane's admitted refresh was re-observed and normalized before final validation.

The repository now validates logical IDs at active selection, archive query, and
archive closeout boundaries. It rejects numeric-leading IDs, terminal-date IDs,
non-canonical archive names, and duplicate logical archive identities; it does
not provide aliases, redirects, symlinks, or date-based fallback.

This record does not assert candidate land, accepted-root closeout, GitLab
publication, hosted CI, retirement, any foreign Work Lane result, or a repair
of private runtime data. Those transitions require fresh evidence at their own
