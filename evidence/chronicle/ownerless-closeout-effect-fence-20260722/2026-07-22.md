## Safety closure

- Decision bytes are read into one strictly parsed canonical snapshot. The
  snapshot must equal the initially admitted decision, and WCP, fence,
  reservation, receipt, and recovery bindings derive from those same bytes.
- A durable exact receipt after a crash authorizes cleanup only. Retry does not
  rerun WCP, repeat Git/worktree effects, or rewrite the receipt; it re-verifies
  the decision and all effect postconditions first.
- Receipt-first cleanup can converge when reservation unlink completed before a
  crash. The exact receipt supplies only the cleanup binding and cannot mint new
  effect authority.
- A non-zero worktree-remove result is re-observed across target ref,
  registration, and path. Only a fully unchanged target remains
  `reserved_no_effect`; partial or unknown effects stay visible.
- Target-ref and closeout-fence reads are three-state. Only explicit ref absence
  passes effect postconditions. Pre-effect requires the exact present fence;
  recovery accepts an explicitly absent fence only with the exact immutable
  receipt. Unverifiable state fails closed.
- Cleanup releases the exact fence before deleting the visible reservation. An
  exact receipt may converge an already-released fence and already-absent
  reservation; a different fence, mismatched receipt, or drifted postcondition
  blocks.
