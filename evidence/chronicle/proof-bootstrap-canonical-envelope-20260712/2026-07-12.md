## Scope

This record addresses one product-level bootstrap defect: the protected
control-replacement verifier formerly expected a top-level proof `head`, while
native executed `ethos prove --execute --json` results bind the candidate under
their evidence and provenance envelopes.

The repair makes the verifier consume the native executed proof result directly.
It does not add a provider, adopter profile, account, credential, service, or
agent requirement; `yheng-agent-ethos` remains optional and default-off. It does
not modify, land, retire, or clean foreign Work Lanes.

## Evidence Boundary

- A bootstrap verifier remains external to the candidate tree and binds exact
  accepted/candidate heads, control digests, verifier digest, proof digest, and
  one bootstrap decision.
- Native proof acceptance requires `command=prove`, `ok=true`,
  `state=proven`, `data.executed=true`, and the same candidate HEAD in evidence
  and provenance.
- This is local control-replacement evidence only. It does not claim hosted
  enforcement, remote publication, or an independent security boundary.

## Recorded Authoring Evidence

- A real candidate closeout exposed the mismatch: the executed proof was
  `proven` and HEAD-bound, but lacked the verifier's obsolete top-level `head`.
- The focused test is changed to the native product result shape before the
  verifier implementation is updated.
- The completed OpenSpec carrier is archived after its native proof contract,
  focused tests, documentation, and specification delta are recorded. Executed
  proof, parity refresh, candidate land, accepted-root closeout, and local
  publication readiness remain separately verified lifecycle gates; remote
  publication remains deferred.
