# Refresh-base ancestry postcondition — 2026-07-12

## Claim

A Work Lane refresh may report success only when the candidate head captured before replay is an ancestor of the resulting Work Lane head.

## Boundary

This record covers the `refresh-base` transition and its ordinary and parity-projection recovery paths. It does not assert candidate integration, accepted-root closeout, lane retirement, or remote publication.

## Evidence

1. The regression runs the exact failure shape: a façade-injected Git runner returns zero for `rebase` without advancing `HEAD`; the pre-fix source returns success, while the repaired source returns `blocked` with `refresh_base_postcondition_failed`.
1. `tests/unit/lanes/test_lanes_lifecycle.py` and `tests/unit/cli/test_contracts_lane_refresh_base_projection.py` pass (27 tests), covering normal refresh, false-success rejection, and projection recovery.
1. `tools/ci/scripts/run-python-lint.sh` passes under the repository-owned Ruff configuration and ratchet.
1. `ethos openspec --change refresh-base-postcondition-20260712 --json` validates the change carrier.

## Limits

This is durable implementation evidence. A separate executed proof is required at the committed head before candidate integration; local closeout and remote publication remain distinct lifecycle states.
