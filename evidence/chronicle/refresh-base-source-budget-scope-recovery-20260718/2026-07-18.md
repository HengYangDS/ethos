# Refresh-Base Source-Budget Scope Recovery — 2026-07-18

## Boundary

This record binds only an exact replay of a source-budget proof-scope correction
that `candidate/dev` has already accepted through a separate archived OpenSpec
carrier and archived claim. It does not attest source-budget debt settlement,
allowance changes, arbitrary candidate preference, remote publication, or
foreign-lane authority.

## Required Evidence

The resolver must prove the three conflicted paths, archived claim subject and
carrier targets, and candidate stage-2 global-compression / proof-floor
invariants before it selects candidate content. Any absence is a blocker.

## Follow-Through

A successful refresh remains only a replay transition. Fresh parity evidence,
executed HEAD-bound proof, local candidate landing, accepted closeout, and each
remote action require their own evidence.
