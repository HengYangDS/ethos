# Report Coordination Advisory Chronicle

## Claim

`ethos report` now carries Work Lane coordination advisory signals from the
existing workspace-status read model into its advisory scorecard layer. The
signals remain non-blocking: report `required_gaps` continue to represent
transition-blocking failures, while Work Lane residue and missing-lease signals
stay visible as advisory disorder.

## Trigger signal

During multi-agent closeout, `ethos status` and `ethos orient` showed linked
foreign Work Lanes, missing leases, and dirty foreign lanes, while
`ethos report` still showed `advisory_gap_count=0`. That made the accepted-root
scorecard too easy to read as "all quiet" even though coordination residue was
present.

## Net gain

- Makes Work Lane residue visible in the scorecard without creating a new truth
  store or command plane.
- Preserves the existing authority boundary: `workspace_status` owns Work Lane
  coordination truth; `report` remains a reader-view scorecard.
- Keeps concurrent agent work non-blocking unless a real required gap exists.
- Routes known coordination advisories to read-only inspection commands:
  `ethos orient --json` and `ethos lane status --json`.
- Preserves OpenSpec accepted-spec obligations by adding one scenario instead of
  replacing earlier advisory-signal scenarios.
