# Typed semantic-attestation receipt record — 2026-07-14

## Boundary

This change corrects assurance vocabulary. It does not assert that any legacy
claim received independent semantic review. Existing `semantic` and
`semantic_attested` labels were demoted to `digest_only` unless a future claim
supplies a fresh typed receipt.

## Contract

New `semantic_attested` claims bind a candidate-external receipt id, file
digest, semantic scope digest, and current HEAD. The receipt binds claim id,
dated evidence digest, reviewer role/ref, basis, allow verdict, validity,
canonical payload digest, and `mints_authority = false`.

## Negative boundary

`digest_only` remains available without a receipt directory, account, daemon,
credential, network, or `yheng-agent-ethos`. No remote publication, hosted CI,
or authority transfer occurred.
