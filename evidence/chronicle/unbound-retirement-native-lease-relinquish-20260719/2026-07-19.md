# Native unbound-retirement lease relinquishment — implementation carrier

event: change/unbound_retirement_native_lease_relinquish
change_id: unbound-retirement-native-lease-relinquish-20260719

## Fact

The exceptional unbound-retirement route previously required no active lease
while its postconditions also required the lease to be absent.  A source holder
with an exact still-live lease could not close that route without leaving the
native lifecycle boundary.

## Decision

The owned carrier implements one narrow continuation: after ordinary
exceptional admission and a no-clobber attempt record, the command may revoke
only the current invocation holder's exact observed lease ID, epoch, and target
head through the existing local CAS.  It re-observes the remaining retirement
bindings before the separate compare-and-delete ref effect, and records the
exact relinquishment binding and result in its durable records.

## Boundary

This is neither generic lease deletion nor retirement authority for another
lane.  A missing, foreign, malformed, stale, replaced, or head-mismatched lease
blocks.  Raw Git or SQLite deletion, force worktree removal, remote mutation,
and hosted or publication claims remain outside this carrier.

## Non-claim

This Chronicle records carrier intent and scope only.  It does not claim focused
tests, OpenSpec archive, proof, land, accepted closeout, source-ref retirement,
remote publication, or hosted CI execution.
