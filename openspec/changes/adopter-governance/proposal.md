## Why

Equivalent inline and registry adopter profiles receive different governance:
inline profiles can accept contradictory protected roles, while registry
profiles can reject their own installed runtime as a foreign checkout. Gate
storage does not determine repository identity or applicable obligations.

## What Changes

- Evaluate common binding, OpenSpec, commit and declared release-role obligations
  independently of how an adopter stores its proof gates.
- Separate ETHOS product conformance from generic repository audit through
  explicit provider composition, preserving trusted prior verification.
- Derive runner and schema provenance from their actual owners and admit the
  exact selected package runtime without requiring adopter-local ETHOS schemas.
- Remove heuristic dispatch and synthetic passing observations; retain precise
  missing, invalid and unobserved results at public admission boundaries.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `repository-governance`: subject=adopter-governance; reuse=extend; change=modify

## Impact

Existing audit, release configuration, schema/runtime binding, proof-provider
and admission owners, their tests and the canonical terminal plan. Public
status, prewrite, proof and integration must agree on applicable obligations.

## Out Of Scope

No adopter or remote mutation, additional binding carrier, repository-name
exception, new lifecycle, proposal replacement, historical identity application,
or dependency replacement. Supply freshness remains a separate existing plan
obligation; this repair is qualified against the locked current supply.
