## Intended Model

Adopters will declare non-empty material governance patterns. Each changed
material path will require coverage by at least one valid ETHOS companion beside
an official active or archiving Change. Missing unrelated companions will remain
diagnostics, not global failures. Bootstrap must admit only the exact new
Change-local companion before later material writes.

## Admission

Implementation begins only with its own RED tests, profile/scaffold contract,
and a source-budget-compliant design; no repo-private script or Superpowers
state may become the carrier.
