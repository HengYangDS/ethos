# Design

`ethos report` derives advisory next actions from explicit advisory gap strings
already admitted by known governance owners. The first supported translation is
protected-branch OpenSpec residue: inspect the branch tree and explain the gap.
The repair itself still requires the owner, handoff, or maintainer break-glass
path; the reader view does not become a mutation authority.

`ethos orient` carries the report advisory next actions into readiness and appends
them after normal transition next actions so humans and agents can act on small
signals without confusing them with blocking required gaps.
