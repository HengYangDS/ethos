## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Tooling adoption closeout preserves ETHOS kernel boundaries

ETHOS SHALL close the tooling adoption plan by binding completed mechanisms to
repository evidence while keeping external tools, providers, method packs, MCP,
and adopter runtimes outside the product kernel unless a future accepted
decision explicitly changes their binding class.

#### Scenario: Completed plan is audited

- **WHEN** a maintainer audits the completed tooling adoption closeout
- **THEN** GitHub and GitLab SHALL remain mirror provider projections over one
  governed repository subject
- **AND** local CI fallback and provider emulator evidence SHALL NOT claim hosted
  provider success
- **AND** OpenSpec customization SHALL remain official-compatible by running
  official validation before ETHOS repo-local lifecycle, capability-profile,
  claim-binding, evidence-ref, and archive checks
- **AND** Superpowers, MCP, Nox, Pixi, Pants, task ledgers, Dagger, OSV,
  image/package scanning, external signing, and adopter-domain runtimes SHALL
  remain replaceable adapters or future profile work unless separately promoted
  by repository evidence.
