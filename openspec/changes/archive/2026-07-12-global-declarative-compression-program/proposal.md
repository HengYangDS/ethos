# Global Declarative Compression Foundation

## Why

ETHOS has strong governance boundaries but its maintained executable source keeps
growing across product Python, tests, tools, reports, configuration, templates,
and provider projections. The existing code-size gate protects individual files;
it cannot prove that a migration removed repository-wide procedure. This carrier
therefore records the completed foundation and selected vertical slices only. The
terminal program remains governed by the canonical plan and must proceed through
later bounded carriers rather than by keeping one permanently active OpenSpec
change.

## What Changes

- Establish the whole-repository source-budget and compression-debt contract.
- Deliver the first typed declaration/projection compiler and CEL topology
  cutover, deleting the duplicated builders and old predicate chain they replace.
- Reconcile the integrated candidate train against the declared debt budget and
  expose the receipt verifier as a public adapter boundary.
- Record that later carrier admission, provider execution, lifecycle, command,
  test/tool/template, and terminal-settlement work remains unimplemented rather
  than treating this foundation as terminal completion.

## Impact

The program affects quality policy, tool declarations, CI projections, package
development dependencies, declaration contracts, policy/read-model compilers,
tests, templates, and documentation. Repository truth and the ETHOS lifecycle
remain owned by ETHOS; external tools are bounded mechanism providers.

## Out Of Scope

- T1, T2, the unfinished portions of T3--T5, and T6--T9 lifecycle, command,
  test/tool/template, and terminal-deletion work.
- A claim that either terminal source budget is met or that all temporary debt is
  settled.
- A workflow platform, scheduler, hosted control plane, OPA/Rego, NetworkX,
  effect/DI, persistent collection, or configuration-language runtime in the
  ETHOS kernel.
- Source-budget evasion by moving code to TOML, CEL, Jinja, generated output, or
  uncounted test/tool paths.
