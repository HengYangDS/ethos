## Why

The source-budget report measures a repository-wide compression program. It is
currently in the default promotion proof, so an unrelated fine-grained Change
cannot land while an already-visible global debt delta awaits its own deletion
wave. That conflates local behavioral admission with program closeout.

## What Changes

- Keep `ethos quality source-budget --json` strict, visible, and unchanged as
a global compression report.
- Remove `source-budget` from the default per-Change promotion proof floor.
- Keep `source-budget` in the full proof and surface it as a distinct global
compression advisory in the scorecard rather than as a local Change-proof failure.

## Capabilities

- `quality`: subject=source-budget-proof-scope; reuse=extend; change=modify;
  facet:lifecycle=validation,proof,closeout; facet:surface=quality,report,
  openspec,evidence; facet:authority=source,test,openspec,claim,evidence.

## Impact

- Gate declaration, scorecard projection, quality specification, tests, and the
  active direct-absorption closeout plan.

## Out Of Scope

- Raising an allowance, baseline, terminal target, or debt cap.
- Excluding source carriers from measurement or treating a global budget gap as
  resolved.
- Remote publication, hosted CI claims, or source-lane retirement.
