## Context

The r3 history is retained as evidence, including ordinary merge ancestry and a dated remote input set. The active r5 continuation owns only the unfinished lifecycle stages and explicitly distinguishes local proof, local protected-ref movement, remote mutation, remote observation, and hosted-provider observation.

## Design

The continuation binds the existing episode claim to a new active OpenSpec change. It first reconstructs the committed history in an owner-bound Lane, refreshes parity and executes a head-bound proof, then applies governed candidate and accepted-root transitions. A candidate-external verifier is used when control replacement rules require it. Per-ref dry-runs precede no-force updates; fresh remote and hosted observations are stored separately. Finally, the carrier is updated, validated, archived, and the Lane is retired.

## Alternatives

- Reopen r3: rejected because an archived promoted delta must not falsely mark later work complete.
- Adopt foreign r4 dirt: rejected because it lacks an owner lease and is not durable input.

## Proof Strategy

Run lifecycle and claims checks, generic parity refresh, a HEAD-bound executed proof, governed land and closeout, per-ref push dry-runs and accepted ordinary updates, fresh provider observations, final archive validation, and final proof.
