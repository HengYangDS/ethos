## Why

The product checkout still carried tracked work notes, mixed-generation rules,
dead assistant and release projections, duplicated quality machinery, and
provider executables that blurred the product/operator boundary. The predecessor
plan also mixed those tracked changes with proposed real `.ethos/state`
maintenance and source-budget terminal settlement that this continuation did not
execute or prove.

This successor change therefore closes only the tracked and testable residue. It
adds conservative local-state capabilities without applying them to real state,
preserves historical claims unless an explicit retirement is recorded, and
reports source-budget campaign progress without claiming terminal compression
completion.

## What Changes

- Remove tracked work notes that explicitly declare themselves non-truth and
  align product configuration with adopter scaffold templates.
- Convert `.ethos/rules.toml` through a lossless, fail-closed Rules V2 migration
  while preserving active quality, gate, source-budget, and evidence policy.
- Retire the root assistant projection and dead release fields without creating
  replacement compatibility files.
- Add and test transactional SQLite v2 migration, conservative lease inventory,
  ref-bound proof retention, and digest-bound archive/maintenance capabilities;
  do not apply them to real local state in this change.
- Retire bundled independent-verification and control-replacement executables
  while preserving provider-neutral receipt contracts and operator-supplied
  adapter boundaries.
- Retire the non-authoritative same-machine performance evidence stack and the
  custom structural blank-line gate while preserving the actual hard quality
  floor.
- Preserve 29 historical claims whose deletion lacked retirement evidence;
  narrow the active assurance claim and archive the two claims whose bundled
  provider implementations were intentionally retired.
- Strengthen closeout admission for unavailable control diffs, control-path
  deletion or rename, candidate-HEAD drift, and candidate-local bootstrap
  artifacts.
- Record exact focused tests, strict OpenSpec validation, claim validation,
  source-budget observation, parity, and HEAD-bound proof as separate evidence.

## Out Of Scope

- Any real `.ethos/state` apply, live SQLite migration, lease or proof pruning,
  recovery archive creation, or recovery-source deletion.
- Source-budget terminal settlement, baseline or terminal-target changes, debt
  policy changes, expiry rollover, or claims of global compression completion.
- Deletion of historical claims without a claim-specific supersession or
  retirement record.
- Remote push, hosted CI, hosted publication, credentials, or provider-side
  mutation as part of this OpenSpec change.
- A new truth store, assistant authority surface, compatibility shim, or second
  governance command plane.

## Impact

Affected tracked surfaces include `.ethos/*.toml`, adopter scaffold templates,
rule migration, local-state implementation and fixtures, proof retention,
quality command registration, release policy, assistant projections, provider
receipt contracts, tests, canonical documentation, claims, Chronicle evidence,
and these OpenSpec deltas. The local-state code is exercised only against
fixtures or copied temporary state; no tracked record may imply that real local
leases, proofs, databases, or recovery material changed. Source-budget remains
an independently visible campaign-terminal observation rather than a correctness
proxy or completion claim for this bounded change.
