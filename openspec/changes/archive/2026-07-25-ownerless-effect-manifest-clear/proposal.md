## Why

Three clean ownerless lanes were semantically absorbed, then removed through
transient exact preservation because direct retirement correctly failed closed
on diverged history. Their branches and worktrees are absent, while the
three verified packages remain. Those packages are recovery bridges rather than
product archives and now require a separate accepted exact-manifest clearance
carrier.

## What Changes

- Bind the three exact decision IDs, completion receipt IDs and digests,
  manifest SHA-256 values, repository bundle SHA-256 values, and empty patch
  payloads.
- Authorize three individual native `lane_resolution/clear-preservation`
  transitions only after this carrier's local accepted closeout.
- Retain every immutable decision and completion receipt, emit one clear receipt
  per package, and leave valid-owner Work Lanes observe-only.

source reconstruction, historical implementation replay, valid-owner Work Lane
mutation, remote publication, or hosted-CI claim.
