## Why

ETHOS has received sustained product guidance across the current task, its
predecessor, and adopter evidence, but the recovery audit remained in
`/private/tmp` and only part of its meaning had reached repository authority.
That makes valid intent vulnerable to session loss while also inviting a second
feedback ledger if the temporary inventory is copied wholesale into Git.

## What Changes

- Define one lossless intake and adjudication path from human guidance and
  external observations into the existing semantic owners.
- Make the Product Design Contract the sole current owner of accepted product
  meaning and the Terminal Governance Product Design the sole current owner of
  unfinished convergence order and exit evidence.
- Use the official OpenSpec Change as the bounded carrier for recovery scope,
  conflicts, decisions, and task completion; archive it after the accepted
  meaning has moved to its final owners.
- Define explicit treatment for direct guidance, delegated observations,
  proposed remedies, implementation state, historical records, and temporary
  working material.
- Require coverage closure across the recovered semantic domains without
  preserving raw conversations, a requirement ledger, a registry, or a second
  roadmap in the repository.
- Distinguish accepted product meaning from unverified implementation state and
  explicitly retire superseded models rather than preserving them as
  compatibility semantics.
- Retire temporary recovery files once their source boundary, adjudication, and
  destination coverage have been verified.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `repository-governance`: Require lossless intent preservation through the
  existing product contract, terminal plan, official OpenSpec lifecycle, and
  executable owners without a parallel semantic store.

- `openspec/specs/repository-governance/spec.md`

This Change implements only two narrow repository guards: the terminal plan is
required by repository audit, and `ledger` is rejected as a documentation role.
It does not claim to implement the recovered authority-resolution, lifecycle,
runtime, publication, physical-layout, or cleanup behavior. It restores their
accepted meaning and establishes the bounded mechanism by which later Changes
must close those gaps.
