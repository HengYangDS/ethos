# Proposal

## Why

On 2026-11-18 the owner asked four things: whether `proposal/*` is a standard form for a branch
name and whether a consensus standard exists; whether the issue and change templates are good
practice, above all for work that coding agents do; what one repository can do on each of the
two forges and what ETHOS uses of it; and which mechanisms of comparable projects are worth
taking. The answers were read from primary sources and from the two forge repositories' own
state. Two designs now rest on them: the record that declares branch names, and the design of
the change surfaces. Until the readings are in the tree, those designs cite a session's working
files.

## What Changes

- `docs/research/change-surfaces/` holds eight studies and their index: branch names; issue and
  change templates; peers; the forges' capabilities; a map of the two forges by purpose; a
  reading of the first three together; the peers' product mechanisms; and which of those
  mechanisms are worth taking, with what was measured for each.
- The research index gains the topic's line.

Not breaking: documentation only. The studies are research: inputs, not accepted behavior.
