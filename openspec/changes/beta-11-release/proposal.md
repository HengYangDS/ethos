# Proposal

## Why

The ten gate rows of 0.2.0-beta.11 are archived on `dev` under passed full proofs of the landed
trees, and the tag is due on 2026-11-21. A release's execution plan is the plan's rows; this
Change keeps the release's own steps, opened now so that none waits in an archived follow-up.

- `adopt-pin` withdrew two tasks under DR-0016. The release build's install smoke is a step of
  this release. The form of the relock is decided, and `adopt-lock-directory` lands it.
- The pre-tag copies run is read by a session other than the batch's authors'. Its preview covered
  13 of 13 readable trees with no new required gap and no new refusal under a proof; its executed
  half had seven full proofs passed on 2026-11-15, the rest in their owners' windows.
- The owner decided on 2026-11-15, as relayed by the adoption session, that the 14-day artifact
  retention ships in this release with its corrected entry.
- The batch carried three off-gate rows against the rule of one a week: the supply move, the
  structure design and the artifact retention. They were in the stack before the rule was set. The
  owner accepted the excess once on 2026-11-15; from 0.2.0-beta.12 a batch carries one.

## What Changes

- Move the product version and turn `[Unreleased]` into the release entry, read against the copies
  run: each Breaking entry names its repair.
- Qualify the release head on both forges, every platform and Python version, as the release guide
  requires.
- The release build's install smoke records three planned files in its brownfield stage and passes
  its MCP stage.
- The copies run's executed half is complete, or each tree it could not prove is named with its
  reason.
- The weekly page's first edition is read from `dev` by a session other than the authors'. Its
  measure of off-gate rows states the three rows of this batch and the owner's acceptance.
