# Proposal

## Why

The adopter bump moves a governed repository's ETHOS selection through one admitted patch
(`adopter-bump`). The weekly page measures the files a bump still writes by hand, and the reading
that the committee of 2026-11-14 took, relayed and not measured again, is 41 of 79 files over 7
bump lanes. By the preparation of `adopter-bump`, 32 of the 41 are files of the bump's own Change
record and 9 are Markdown, the changelog line among them. No pin mover touches either kind, so
`adopter-bump` alone does not bring the measure to its target of 0.

The archived `product-form-plan` design gives the bump its full scope: one preview and apply in a
lane that changes the pin, relocks, previews the paradigm repair and the projection check, and
adds the changelog line. The release lead ruled on 2026-11-15 that the record and the changelog
line are a row of their own, so that the pin's row lands at its measured size.

## What Changes

Candidates, none decided:

- The bump writes its own Change record from the release it moves to: the proposal from the
  release entry's lines that name an adopter's repair, the tasks from the steps the bump names.
- The bump adds the changelog line; a writer for it measured 43.5 product line-equivalents in the
  first preparation of the bump.
- Or a ruling that a pin-only bump needs no Change record of its own. The gate's item on ceremony
  says today that a pin-only bump does not take the lean path, so such a ruling changes that item
  and is the owner's.

The paradigm repair's plan and the changelog owner. A bump without a Change record changes what
