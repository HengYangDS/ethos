# Proposal

## Why

`direction-carriers` was signed by its three review seats on 2026-11-15. Its second step is text
only: the sentences of law that kept the existing carriers from being used. Inputs that sessions
of governed repositories sent the same day:

- A design rule that two repositories added to their OpenSpec configuration word for word: rest
  every statement about platform behavior, another program or concurrency on a named measurement
  or on a test that forces the order, and list the cases of every classification before the
  decision that rests on them.
- A study's shape that worked for such a case: questions; a method that can be repeated; findings,
  each with its source and counts; what the findings decided, one row from finding to decision
  with the measured alternative that was not taken; limits.
- A research lifecycle with five states and a dossier template, which one repository keeps in its
  lane's history.

## What Changes

- The contract's and the plan's sentences on transcripts and memory become the session rule's
  sentence, and the contract stays at 500 nonblank lines.
- The plan's rules of convergence gain the sentence that a draft is open by rule.
- DR-0016 gains a successor sentence: a withdrawn task's draft exists at the withdrawal.
- The OpenSpec configuration's rules: a follow-up that is work names its draft; each decision of a
  design carries its four parts; the withdrawal rule and the agent rules follow.
- The research role's page in the documentation contract: a study's parts, its two kinds of source
  and its review date as text; the reader comes later.
