## Why

A historical source-policy Work Lane still holds three dirty test files. Its
committed history is accepted, but passing temporary probes do not prove that
every useful assertion survives in maintained tests. Retirement must not erase
the only executable record of a valid boundary.

## What Changes

- Compare the complete dirty test increment with current semantic owners.
- Absorb missing package-boundary cases into existing tests, without restoring
  replaced contributor-role policy or duplicating reference and budget tests.
- Record the precise preservation decision and remaining retirement boundary in
  the existing terminal plan.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

None. This is regression preservation for existing behavior and uses official
`skip_specs: true`; no artificial requirement delta is introduced.

## Impact

Existing product-boundary tests and the terminal plan change. Product code,
dependencies, runtime semantics, quality thresholds, and public APIs do not.
The historical dirty root remains unchanged. A separate admitted effect must
establish its safe disposal; this Change does not grant authoring permission in
an invalid historical intent, force-remove a worktree, or claim retirement.
