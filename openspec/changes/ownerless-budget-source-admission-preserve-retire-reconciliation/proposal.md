## Why

Fresh target-scoped direct-retire decisions for two clean ownerless budget
predecessors now reach the intended native boundary. Each apply returns only
`lane_resolution_ownerless_target_not_accepted_ancestor` and leaves package,
receipt, ref, worktree, fence, reservation, lease, and accepted state unchanged.
The sources remain redundant through five valid-owner descendants, but semantic
retention does not make them ancestors of accepted truth.

## What Changes

- Preserve both direct-retire decisions and exact no-effect results as immutable
  records; never rewrite or reuse them.
- Add one accepted target-bound `lane_resolution/preserve-retire` Chronicle and
  digest-only Claim for each exact source branch and HEAD.
- Bind both target Claims to this one OpenSpec authority carrier while keeping
  their effect admission independent and serial.
- Supersede only the two consumed target-scoped direct-retire Claims.
- After official archive, exact-HEAD proof, candidate land, and accepted
  closeout, create two new decisions and execute the native effects serially.
- Retain each exact package, bundle, manifest, and completion receipt; package
  clear remains a separately accepted exact-manifest transition.
