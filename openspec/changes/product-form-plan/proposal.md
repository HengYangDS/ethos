# Proposal

## Why

On 2026-11-12 the owner asked for stronger product value, a clear unique advantage, community reach
and a product form that is genuinely usable by agents first and people second, and delegated the
judgment. A landscape study of 41 products and a newcomer agent's first-run audit found the kernel
sound and the product unable to show it: no install step, a starter that fails its own proof, a
hidden normal path and evidence that never leaves the maintainer's machine. Five reviewers then
converged on one product-form design over three rounds, with no rejection, and the owner decided the
open questions (D1 to D7) the same day. The design lives in ignored scratch, so no reviewer, gate or
later lane reads it; the terminal plan names no place in its order for the first hour, the agent
budgets or the adopter bump.

## What Changes

- The terminal plan gains a short section, `Product Form And First Hour`, after
  `0.2.0 Release Candidate Order`, that adds to the RC order without a new family: the gating agent
  budgets join A17's acceptance (owner decision D2); the adopter bump becomes its own RC Change
  after C3 (D7); the first-hour Changes depend only on what is on `dev`, with A7 before N1; the
  landing-attestation export is a 0.3.0 direction that starts with a contract amendment. It cites
  this Change's design.
- This Change's design records the converged product form: the position and its three stated limits,
  the neighbours, the public normal path, the agent budgets and their owners, the first hour, the
  export direction, the channels, the footprint, the fleet bump, the community gates, the order, and
  the owner's decisions.
- Out of scope: every implementation, specification and test change; each follows as its own Change
  that cites this design.
