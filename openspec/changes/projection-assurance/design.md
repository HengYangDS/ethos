## Context

The accepted product contract owns meaning. The semantic graph already names
43 source-backed invariant propositions, the view selects their witnesses, and
the quality contract owns acceptance values. R/P lists introduced during the
original projection import have no definitions in the inspected original owner
or current consumers. Their removal must preserve underlying obligations rather
than fabricate an equivalence with those opaque names.

## Decision

Reuse the graph's invariant identities and exact source principles. The quality
owner groups their evidence requirements by semantic failure boundary, without
copying their propositions. Every required invariant has exactly one defined
acceptance family; each family states its subject/scope, evidence and a concrete
rejection condition. Principle review refers to the original exact source.

Source declaration validation, rendered mechanical observation, independent
semantic review and product-owner acceptance are separate claims. Reports bind
the selected source, quality contract, exact artifact, medium, verifier and
evidence. An ID, hash, passing report or shared implementation is insufficient
to establish faithfulness or independent identity.

A static overview must carry required meaning in its visible view or an
intelligible aggregate. A multipage atlas may distribute meaning only through
explicit visible navigation and bounded scope; unvisited pages or hover do not
establish static coverage. Every medium preserves the full declared source scope
and reports actual omissions. A scoped story, Present view, share or export
preserves the necessary conditions of every relationship it expresses without
claiming whole-product coverage. Hidden document prose cannot repair a misleading
current frame. Geometry checks remain medium-specific.

A predecessor is a comparison floor only when exact artifacts, source scope,
applicable policy, observed evidence and accepted review are identified.
A current candidate cannot lower the policy to qualify itself. When no qualified
predecessor exists, absolute current acceptance still applies; neither improvement
nor regression is inferred from an unqualified predecessor. Historic observations
remain readable and bounded, never silently reused as current permission.

## Implementation Boundary

Extend the existing projection policy and exporter instead of introducing a
second graph or assurance engine. The isolated committed exporter must remain
runnable without source-checkout imports; use a standard-library-compatible
JSON carrier at the existing quality owner and delegate validation to selected source.
Rename the YAML carrier to JSON, removing the old path and updating its declaration.
Consumers that already parse YAML also accept this JSON document; their exact
source import must still be refreshed. Unknown or malformed versions fail closed. Existing immutable v3 inputs
remain historical; the updated contract has an explicit consumer interpretation
boundary.

## Validation

First show that undefined required checks and uncovered graph invariants can
reach successful export. Add rejection cases through the actual immutable-tree
exporter for missing proposition references, duplicate ownership, unknown source
references and absent faithfulness or predecessor rules. Verify complete valid
source export, standard-library isolation, determinism and unchanged meaning,
including all graph invariants and static witnesses.

No completeness claim is made for arbitrary natural-language interpretation.
The declaration gate proves well-defined obligations; renderer evidence and
independent meaning review must still satisfy them before final acceptance.

## Delivery Order

1. Admit official intent and record current accepted delivery in the existing plan.
2. Establish RED, repair the one owner and remove superseded identifier lists.
3. Run focused counterexamples, source export and inexpensive native quality checks.
4. Freeze and prove exact source, archive and reprove, accept, install and publish.
5. Return the exact export identity and migration boundary to the renderer owner;
   retire this lane after preserving only evidence with a current consumer.
