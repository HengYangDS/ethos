## Why

The terminal visual-quality contract requires R01–R14 and P00–P09 results but
provides no propositions behind those identifiers. The original source import
and current renderer owners retain only the lists. Exact export therefore
certifies bytes while leaving required acceptance undefined. Geometry, graph
counts and a prior candidate's PASS cannot close that gap.

## What Changes

- Replace undefined parallel check identifiers with obligations referencing the
  existing semantic graph and source principles. Declare scope, required
  observations and distinguishing rejection conditions at the quality owner.
- **BREAKING**: use one JSON quality contract and validate its complete assurance
  declaration before export. Missing,
  unknown, duplicate and uncovered obligations fail at this inexpensive boundary.
- Define medium-specific faithfulness, independent review and qualified or absent
  predecessor behavior without adding product components or another workflow.
- Preserve exact exporter isolation, source binding and all existing geometric
  quality floors. Keep report completeness distinct from evidence validity and
  actual owner acceptance.
- Update the existing canonical plan with delivery facts, dispositions and the
  remaining public runtime-update discovery obligation.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `quality`: subject=projection-assurance; reuse=extend; change=modify

## Impact

The existing terminal quality contract, exporter and its policy/test owners
change. Both renderers consume one exported contract after source delivery.
No adopter, renderer, package dependency, persistent claim database or parallel
roadmap is introduced.

## Out Of Scope

- Renderer geometry, interaction, visual composition and owner aesthetic approval.
- Whole-proof reuse, publication latency and runtime latest-release discovery implementation.
- Reinterpreting historic undefined report IDs as proof of current semantics.
