## Why

Its requirements are accepted when it archives to `openspec/changes/archive/2026-10-17-proof-throughput`; this Change carries its open implementation obligations 1.9, 2.12, 2.13, 2.14, 9.1, 9.2, 9.3 for one cohesive outcome. ## What Changes

- Preserve original task identities, truthful states, acceptance conditions, dependencies and evidence references; conjoin both parents' remaining obligations without restoring superseded duties.
