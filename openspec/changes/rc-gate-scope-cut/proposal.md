# Proposal

## Why

DR-0029 holds the gate of 0.2.0 as 43 rows with five weekly tags. Design studies of 2026-11-17 for
the batch of 0.2.0-beta.13 found that the plan's rows are larger than the sizes the plan gave them:
of nine studied rows only a first slice fits its batch, and the first slices alone pass the test
ceiling. The owner sent the question to the committee on 2026-11-17. Its five seats signed one
resolution unanimously in their third round on the same day, and the owner answered its five asks
that evening, each with the committee's recommendation. DR-0029 says that a later waiver, a row
that leaves or a scope cut is a successor record that names it. This Change is that record.

The committee's resolution and its votes are local documents of the releasing checkout; the text
below is their tracked form. Most sizes behind it are estimates of the studies, and the forecast
for 2026-12-05 is an inference: `dev` reads 22 to 24 rows against a line of 15, on which DR-0029's
slip rule moves the freeze one week.

## What Changes
