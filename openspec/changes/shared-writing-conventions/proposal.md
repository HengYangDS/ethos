# Proposal

## Why

The owner directed on 2026-11-14 that what governed repositories share sinks into the common
base. One governed repository offered three conventions of its own on 2026-11-15, kept in its
history at one named commit; the release lane holds a copy with its checksums as local evidence:

- A style guide for design documents and diagrams, 706 lines.
- A research lifecycle of five states with a dossier template, 105 and 60 lines. The direction
  design took the lifecycle as an input to the research role.
- Two wording rules for the prose checker: one refuses relative time terms and asks for absolute
  dates or defined limits; one refuses vague qualifiers of execution and asks for the trigger or
  the condition.

ETHOS's own prose rules hold neither wording rule, and its documentation contract has no style
guide for diagrams.

## What Changes

Candidates, none decided:

- The two wording rules join the prose rules that ETHOS ships, first as reports.
- The parts of the style guide that are no repository's own taste become a short page of the
  documentation contract.
- The dossier template becomes the research role's template, if the direction design's research
  page does not already cover it.
