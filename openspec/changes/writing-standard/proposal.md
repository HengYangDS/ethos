# Proposal

## Why

The owner directed on 2026-11-21 that the standard of writing, fidelity, clarity and elegance
(`信、达、雅`), has one definition, which every carrier cites and none rewords. His shared rules
hold it. In this repository the Product Design Contract carried a gloss of its own: five
adjectives and a sentence on elegance.

On the same day he directed that a fault in a source is repaired at the source. A session that
drafts from ETHOS's documents reported twenty places that fail the standard. Read at the head,
eight are faults in documents that this repository's keeper repairs; four more stand in contract
text and wait for the owner's word; the rest are not faults, are closed, or are the standard
itself.

## What Changes

- A governance document, the Writing Standard, holds the definition copied word for word from
  the owner's shared rules. A test holds the digest of its words.
- The Product Design Contract cites that document in place of its gloss and keeps its own rule
  that visual appeal never permits a second ontology or an unverified completion claim.
- Eight repairs: the brand kit's path takes the contract's members; four documents state their
  own purpose where each carried the same sentence; one paragraph of 58 lines in the runner
  document becomes nine; a verdict sentence there separates its three clauses; the README drops
  a sentence that repeats its tagline; two glossary entries say what they define; one sentence
  of the learning document no longer reads as done.

Not here: the four places in contract text (what "no rival authority" means, the positioning
sentence, a sentence that stands between two subjects, and an invariant that disagrees with the
verdict union).

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

## Impact

`docs/governance/` (a new document, the index and three lines of the contract),
`docs/architecture/` (three documents), `docs/reference/` (the brand kit and the glossary), the
README and one architecture test. No command, schema or verdict changes.
